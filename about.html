ABOUT VIRASAT

The confluence of timeless royal heritage, thoughtful hospitality, and premier celebration spaces in Udhampur.

OUR HERITAGE & VISION

Our Story

Virasat Suites & Banquets was envisioned by the K.N. Group to establish a benchmark of refined hospitality in Udhampur, Jammu & Kashmir. Rooted in the rich cultural heritage of Northern India, "Virasat" translates to legacy — an emblem of honored traditions, gracious hosting, and lasting memories.

Situated prominently along the NH1A corridor in Shiv Nagar, adjacent to Mahindra Asto India, Virasat was designed from the ground up to address a vital need: a dignified venue where discerning travelers find tranquility, and families can host life's most momentous milestones in regal grandeur.

“We set out to create more than just a hotel; we built a home for cherished celebrations and quiet comfort.”

THE ART OF SERVICE

Hospitality Philosophy

At Virasat, our philosophy is anchored in the classic principle of heartfelt Indian hospitality — treating every guest with warmth, genuine attentiveness, and understated luxury.
